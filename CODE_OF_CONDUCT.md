# Community standards

SliceMe welcomes practical, respectful participation from people of different skill levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Give reproducible technical feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy, licensing, trademarks, and third-party project boundaries.
- Clearly disclose relevant affiliations, automated contributions, and generated material.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused enough to review and verify.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, fabricated evidence, credential sharing, and publishing another person's private models or data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious security or privacy concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public discussion.

If you believe moderation is needed, use GitHub's report/blocking tools or open a concise issue without repeating harmful or sensitive content.
